Health & Importance
🌽
Maize (Corn)
Zea mays
One of the world's most important staple crops, maize feeds both humans and livestock across every continent. Healthy maize shows deep green, erect leaves with firm stalks. Yellowing of lower leaves often signals nitrogen deficiency — early detection saves entire harvests.
Staple High Yield Water Sensitive
Health Index
82%
🍅
Tomato
Solanum lycopersicum
A globally consumed fruit crop that demands consistent soil moisture and warm temperatures. Tomato plants show stress through leaf curl, blossom drop, and dark patches. Early blight and late blight spread rapidly — daily visual scouting with AI detection can prevent losses of up to 70%.
High Value Disease Prone Moisture Critical
Health Index
74%
🌾
Rice
Oryza sativa
Rice feeds over half the world's population, making its health monitoring one of the highest-impact applications in precision agriculture. Brown spot disease and bacterial blight are responsible for billions in annual losses — both detectable in early stages through canopy color changes captured by rover cameras.
Global Staple Flood Tolerant Blight Sensitive
Health Index
91%
🥜
Groundnut
Arachis hypogaea
A drought-tolerant legume and critical protein source across sub-Saharan Africa. Groundnuts fix nitrogen into soil, improving land for future crops. Aflatoxin contamination — caused by drought stress — is a major health and economic risk, making early moisture monitoring essential.
Legume Drought Tolerant Soil Enriching
Health Index
88%
🥦
Cassava
Manihot esculenta
The caloric backbone of West and Central Africa, cassava is a hardy root crop that tolerates poor soils. Cassava Mosaic Virus — transmitted by whiteflies — causes mosaic yellowing on leaves visible from above. Rover-based image analysis can flag infected plants before the disease spreads.
Root Crop Hardy Virus Sensitive
Health Index
79%
🧅
Pepper
Capsicum annuum
Bell and hot peppers are high-value cash crops with narrow tolerances for temperature and moisture extremes. Pepper plants show early heat stress through leaf curl and bleaching. Consistent soil moisture between 60–80% is critical — exactly what this rover monitors autonomously.
Cash Crop Heat Sensitive High Value
Health Index
85%
